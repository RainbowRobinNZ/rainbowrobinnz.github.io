About Us:

At Snazzy Paws Grooming, we believe every dog deserves to look and feel their best. Based in Hamilton, our grooming salon was founded by a couple with a genuine passion for dogs and a commitment to providing high-quality grooming services to pets and their owners.

From baths and haircuts to nail trims, teeth brushing and specialised treatments, we offer a range of grooming options to suit dogs of all ages and needs. Our friendly team takes pride in providing professional care while making every visit as comfortable and enjoyable as possible for your furry friend.
